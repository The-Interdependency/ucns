# UCNS metadata-free relational carrier

Status: bounded, non-geometric representation bridge. It is not a complete
`UCNSObject`, placement law, higher-gonol product, equivalence rule, measurement,
or theorem.

`ucns.relational_carrier` accepts only a dense ordered integer occurrence set and
an ordered sequence of directed integer relation triples. It preserves node
order, edge order, multiplicity, relation codes, endpoints, and sidedness. Its
canonical JSON contains no words, source labels, meanings, corpus provenance, or
domain metadata.

A domain producer owns a separate identity binding from its exact evidence to
the dense addresses and relation codes. The producer must freeze that binding
and the intrinsic carrier independently. Hashes may identify those artifacts;
they do not derive coordinates or geometry.

The bridge intentionally supplies no angle, coordinate, carrier placement,
Möbius transition, circle/epicycle/disk/sphere law, `M`, `B`, or validity
transfer. Those unresolved UCNS boundaries cannot be filled by a consumer.

hmmm: geometric assignment, higher-gonol composition, canonical structural
equivalence, and domain interpretation of relation codes remain independent
authority and evidence decisions.
