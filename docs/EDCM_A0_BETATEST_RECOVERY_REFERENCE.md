## Corrected root

**UCNS assigns elements of an unknowable to completion through geometric motion.**

The motion algorithm is expressed through epicyclic circles, spheres, and disks. Therefore, UCNS is not fundamentally:

- a static 157-position character carrier;
- a normalized factorization object;
- a hash or identity wrapper;
- a scalar coordinate system;
- a Fourier decomposition; or
- a collection of elements awaiting set comparison.

Those may all be instruments or projections. The system is the assignment, motion, nesting, recursive scale transition, and registration of completion.

Completion must also be scoped. It completes the declared construction relative to its boundary; it does not imply that the underlying unknowable has been epistemically exhausted.

## It remains a prototype of the right shape

Closest fit does not mean mechanically complete.

The inspected A0 implementation still substitutes:

- hash-derived phase lanes for a derived assignment law;
- ordinary 2π angular fractions in several places;
- sine-sign chirality for a complete twist/orientation construction;
- a cylindrical disk stack without the full sphere and epicycle nesting law;
- factorization-derived composition where the relationship to completion motion remains unproved; and

Those substitutions are visible in the code: its phase streams are generated by Blake2 hashes and projected into ordinary circle angles, while the composition operates on quantized angular lanes.

The correct judgment is:

> **a0-betatest contains the nearest recoverable EDCM-facing UCNS architecture, not the finished UCNS algorithm.**

The recovery operation should extract its invariants, not blindly promote all of its temporary mechanisms.

## The six A0 metric families are closer in question-form

The earlier A0 specification framed:

- Constraint Mismatch as relation between declared and observed constraints;
- Dissonance Accumulation as unresolved buildup;
- Drift as movement away from a goal vector;
- Divergence as branching trajectories;
- Intensity as magnitude or rate; and
- Turn Balance as distribution across actors.

Those are closer to the proper questions because they concern relation, accumulation, displacement, branching, magnitude, and distribution. Their existing formulas are still proxies, but their semantic forms can map naturally onto a motion system:

| Metric | Candidate motion-system reading |
|---|---|
| `CM` | relation mismatch affecting admissible motion |
| `DA` | unresolved motion retained across turns |
| `DRIFT` | displacement from the completion trajectory |
| `DVG` | separation into competing epicyclic trajectories |
| `INT` | amplitude or rate of motion |
| `TBF` | distribution of contribution across actors |

That mapping is a candidate specification, not yet canon. It gives a better repair direction than treating eleven scalar axes as the final object.
