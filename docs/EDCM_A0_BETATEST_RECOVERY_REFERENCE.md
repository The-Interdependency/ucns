## Load-bearing system definition

> UCNS assigns elements of an unknowable to completion through a geometric-motion algorithm of epicyclic circles, spheres, and disks.

This definition governs the recovery program.

UCNS is not exhausted by any one object constructor, carrier, character table,
coordinate, hash, factorization kernel, metric vector, or visualization. Those
may be instruments, projections, or implementation surfaces. They do not replace
the system function: assignment through geometric motion to completion.

Completion is relative to the declared construction and its boundary. It does not
mean that the unknowable has been converted into complete knowledge. A recovered
implementation must state what has been assigned, the geometry in which it moves,
the completion condition, and what remains unresolved.

## Terms held for recovery

### Unknowable

The source domain from which an assignment is made without claiming exhaustive
knowledge of that domain.

### Element

A distinction admitted into a declared UCNS construction. Admission must retain
its source identity, scale, ordering, and unresolved relations required by the
construction.

### Assignment

The placement of an element into a geometric relation that constrains its
possible motion and its contribution to completion. Assignment is not mere
indexing or labeling.

### Motion

The ordered geometric alteration of assigned elements through circles,
epicycles, disks, spheres, and recursive relations among those forms. Motion must
retain orientation, nesting, scale, and causal order whenever they affect the
completion result.

### Completion

A registered condition of the declared construction. Completion must identify
its boundary and cannot silently collapse remaining uncertainty into zero,
absence, or success.

## Geometry required by the root

The recovery must account for all of the following without treating one as a
synonym for another:

1. **Circle** — an oriented local carrier or motion relation.
2. **Epicycle** — a circle whose center or frame participates in another motion.
3. **Disk** — the state-bearing interior associated with a circle, not merely its
   circumference.
4. **Sphere** — a higher enclosing or relational surface required by the system,
   not a decorative rendering of a disk.
5. **Recursive scale** — a completed construction may become an assigned element
   within a larger construction.
6. **Möbius origin and hidden zero** — the twist, seam, orientation state, and
   720-degree complete return remain load-bearing.

The exact transition and composition laws are not supplied by this document.
They remain the principal mathematical recovery obligation.
