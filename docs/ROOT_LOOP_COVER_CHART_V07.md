## II. Exact chart hypothesis

The native root-loop state is

\[
(p,\varepsilon),
\qquad
p\in\mathbb Q\cap[0,1),
\qquad
\varepsilon\in\{+1,-1\}.
\]

The exact directed-cover chart coordinate is

\[

\]

measured in visible turns. One turn is 360° and two turns are 720°.

### 2. Forward map

\[
\Phi(p,\varepsilon)=
\begin{cases}
p, & \varepsilon=+1,\\
p+1, & \varepsilon=-1.
\end{cases}
\]

The positive frame maps to the first lifted representative. The reversed frame
maps to the second lifted representative.

### 3. Inverse map

\[
\Phi^{-1}(\alpha)=
\begin{cases}
(\alpha,+1), & 0\le\alpha<1,\\
(\alpha-1,-1), & 1\le\alpha<2.
\end{cases}
\]

Both maps use exact rational arithmetic. Therefore

\[
\Phi^{-1}\!\circ\Phi=\operatorname{id}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\Phi\circ\Phi^{-1}=\operatorname{id}
\]

on the declared root-loop domain.
