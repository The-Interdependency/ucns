## Boundary

This certificate establishes the complete symbolic presentation and the exact
first-nonzero elementary-ideal boundary. It does not enumerate every minor or
claim a reduced generating set or Gröbner basis for `E_1` or `E_3`. A single
nonzero member proves ideal nonvanishing but does not generate the whole ideal.

It also does not establish ambient isotopy, a complete link invariant, unique
prime forcing, a phase-law selection, higher Milnor invariants, or any spectral
or zeta claim. Selection effect remains `none`.
