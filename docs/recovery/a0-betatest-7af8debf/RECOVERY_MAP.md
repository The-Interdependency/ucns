# A0 Beta-Test Recovery Map

## Classification vocabulary

- **Recoverable architecture:** a relation or boundary worth restating as a
  current candidate invariant.
- **Provisional mechanism:** useful executable evidence whose mathematical
  authority remains unearned.
- **Counterevidence:** a concrete mechanism that demonstrates how the intended
  shape was flattened or substituted.
- **Activation hazard:** an archived surface that must remain disabled.

No classification in this packet selects universal UCNS canon or establishes
EDCM measurement validity.

## Cross-file invariants to recover

The packet supports restating these candidate invariants for new work:

1. Exact occurrences precede projections.
2. Motion is ordered and repetition-bearing.
3. Seam or nesting-interface encounters are events, not deletions.
4. Orientation is load-bearing state.
5. Grain is recursive; higher structure does not replace lower evidence.
6. Composition order can change the result.
7. A session state is accumulated from complete turns in source order.
8. Full field state remains available after any scalar projection.
9. Private arrangement may parameterize a construction but cannot replace its
   public provenance or laws.
10. EDCM readouts ask about changes in a trajectory rather than naming a final
    scalar as the trajectory itself.

## Known flattening operations

The historical sources also provide executable counterexamples:

- `_lane_values()` replaces an assignment law with Blake2 bytes.
- `similarity()` and `coherence()` project trajectories into ordinary `2*pi`
  trigonometric scalars.
- `_grain_texts()` lowercases, regex-tokenizes, deduplicates words, joins turns,
  and reparses clauses.
- `PrivateGonal.inscribe()` maps angles through an ordinary one-lap fraction.
- `whiten_payload()` hashes the 53-lane field into 32 bytes.
- `frame_value()` maps continuous values onto arbitrary small denominators.
- `carrier_lcm()` delegates composition to archived multiplication.
- `readout()` reduces text to normalized token sets, density heuristics, and six
  bounded scalars.

These operations remain preserved because negative evidence is part of the
recovery.

## Extraction order

1. Specify exact assignment evidence and occurrence identity.
2. Specify Möbius initiation and 720-degree orientation.
3. Re-express lifted traversal against that carrier.
4. Preserve the full field without hash substitution.
5. Define recursive circle/epicycle/disk/sphere transitions.
6. Define larger-gonol composition over exact word and SPACE evidence.
7. Define completion registration.
8. Bind EDCM events to trajectory changes.
9. Introduce projections only after source-retaining laws exist.
10. Reintroduce no archived arithmetic unless a separately reviewed candidate
    proves it serves completion motion.
