# UCNS Completion-Motion Recovery Root

## Governing statement

UCNS assigns elements of an unknowable to completion through geometric motion.

The motion is expressed through nested and epicyclic circles, disks, and
spheres. The system is therefore the assignment, motion, orientation, nesting,
recursive scale transition, and registration of completion—not any one
carrier fixture, object constructor, hash, factorization, scalar coordinate,
or Fourier decomposition.

Completion closes the declared construction relative to its explicit boundary.
It does not imply that the underlying unknowable has been epistemically
exhausted.

## EDCM-scoped load-bearing constraints

The recovery must preserve the EDCM decisions already recorded in
`docs/UCNS_OPTION_DECISIONS.md` and `src/ucns/option_registry.json`:

1. Structural Null is the singular superpositioned space.
2. A new gonol initiates through the Möbius twist.
3. The twist supplies a causal transition rather than a decorative two-lap
   visualization.
4. The return structure is 720 degrees; a 360-degree visible projection cannot
   erase orientation or invent automatic negation.
5. The word is the smallest EDCM gonol.
6. Exact character tokens compose word gonols.
7. Exact SPACE occurrences remain superpositioned nesting interfaces.
8. Every larger gonol composes from word gonols without flattening those
   interfaces.
9. Occurrences retain order and repetition.
10. Scalar readouts are declared lossy projections of recoverable source
    trajectories, never the complete measurement identity.

These are EDCM-scoped constraints and do not appoint a universal UCNS option
configuration.

## Required construction sequence

A candidate completion-motion implementation must make each transition
explicit:

1. **Boundary declaration** — identify the unknowable boundary and the evidence
   admitted as elements.
2. **Assignment** — assign each admitted element to a provenance-bearing
   geometric relation without normalizing away occurrence identity.
3. **Initiation** — construct the gonol from singular superpositioned Structural
   Null through the Möbius twist.
4. **Orientation** — retain sidedness, chirality, heading, and 720-degree return
   behavior without deriving them from an ordinary `2*pi` convenience.
5. **Motion** — preserve the ordered trajectory, seam/interface events, repeated
   positions, and relative displacement.
6. **Nesting** — specify circle, epicycle, disk, and sphere parentage.
7. **Scale transition** — specify how character evidence composes into word
   gonols and how word gonols compose into larger grains.
8. **Registration** — define the geometric condition that registers completion
   within the declared boundary.
9. **Observation** — bind EDCM evidence to changes in assignment, motion,
   nesting, and remaining unresolved capacity.
10. **Projection** — permit a scalar or summary only with retained source
    trajectory, named policy, and explicit loss.

## Minimum EDCM event identity

An EDCM observation derived from completion motion needs at least:

- observed element and exact source occurrence;
- provenance and declared unresolved boundary;
- grain and parent grain;
- assigned geometric relation;
- orientation and sidedness;
- motion since the prior state;
- epicyclic, disk, or sphere parentage;
- effect on the declared completion condition;
- remaining unresolved capacity;
- measurement standing;
- optional named lossy projections.

The existing six A0 readout families remain candidate questions:

| Family | Recovery question |
|---|---|
| Constraint Mismatch | Which declared relation prevents or redirects admissible motion? |
| Dissonance Accumulation | Which unresolved motion remains retained across turns? |
| Drift | What displacement occurred from the declared completion trajectory? |
| Divergence | Which competing trajectories separated, and where? |
| Intensity | What amplitude or rate of motion was observed? |
| Turn Balance | How was completion-relevant contribution distributed among actors? |

Their historical formulas do not become measurement authority through this
mapping.

## Explicit non-equivalences

The recovery must not equate:

- the 157-position token fixture with the whole UCNS system;
- SPACE, digit `0`, algebraic zero, absent cell, neutral product character, and
  Structural Null;
- a directed twofold cover with the Möbius causal carrier without a formal
  preservation result;
- `2*pi` phase wrapping with the 720-degree return law;
- sine sign with complete chirality;
- hash-derived lanes with the assignment law;
- a cylindrical stack with the complete circle/disk/sphere nesting law;
- order-sensitive factorization with completion motion;
- coherence or six scalar scores with the complete EDCM observation.
